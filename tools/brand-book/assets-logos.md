# Logos

- **krastysoft-black.svg**: the full wordmark (KRASTY over `<SOFT>`) with `logo-white` (#f0f0f0) ink and `logo-red` (#f30000) accents, for black and near-black grounds. The default; the site header uses it. It looks empty on a light tile because its ink is light.
- **krastysoft-white.svg**: the same wordmark in black ink with a grey `SOFT`, for white grounds (print, partner sites).
- **logo.svg**: the A mark alone (`logo-white` arch, `logo-red` triangle), for avatars and small square spaces on dark grounds.
- **app-icon.svg**: the K mark on a `brand-black` tile with a 6px corner (`src/app/icon.svg`), used as the favicon.

Never recolour, outline, add effects to or redraw these files.
